# Bibliography: Environmental Predictors of Archaeological Settlement

*Compiled 2026-04-06 from web search on environmental variables used in settlement location prediction and environment-culture correlation.*

---

## Archaeological Predictive Modeling

- [Settlement strategies and their driving mechanisms of Neolithic settlements using machine learning approaches: a case study in Zhejiang Province](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-025-01753-4) — *npj Heritage Science* (2025). Machine learning on Neolithic sites; water buffer zones, elevation, slope as top predictors.

- [A Prediction Study on Archaeological Sites Based on Geographical Variables and Logistic Regression — Neolithic and Bronze Age of Xiangyang](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/14/23/15675) — *MDPI Sustainability* (2022). Most sites at 0–150m elevation, slope 0–10°, within 1000m of rivers.

- [Mn/Model Final Report Chapter 6: Archaeological and Environmental Variables](https://dot.mn.gov/mnmodel/P3FinalReport/chapter6.html) — Minnesota DOT. Most comprehensive APM found; ~69 variables across terrain, hydrology, soils, vegetation. Introduced watershed variables (ridge distances) in Phase 3.

- [Predictive modeling in geoarchaeology: An evaluation of machine learning algorithms and topographic variables — Serranópolis, Brazil](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2212054824000353) — *ScienceDirect* (2024).

- [Explainable AI for archaeological site prediction in Surkhandarya, Uzbekistan](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-025-02267-9) — *npj Heritage Science* (2025).

- [Predictive modeling for archaeological site locations: Logistic regression and MaxEnt in north Israel and north-east China](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0305440318300293) — *Journal of Archaeological Science* (2018).

- [Geostatistical analysis of historical contingency and land use footprints in prehistoric settlement dynamics, South Carolina Piedmont](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0305440318305673) — *Journal of Archaeological Science* (2018).

## Environment–Culture Correlation

- [The Ecology-Culture Dataset: A new resource for investigating cultural variation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9553914/) — *PMC* (2022). Nine ecological variables (rainfall, temperature, GDP, disease, population density, etc.) correlated with Schwartz cultural values; moderate overall correlation (Baker's gamma 0.26).

- [The archaeology of climate change: a blueprint for integrating environmental and cultural systems](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60450-9) — *Nature Communications* (2025).

- [D-PLACE data sources](https://github.com/D-PLACE/dplace-data/blob/master/SOURCES.md) — GitHub. Authoritative source list for D-PLACE environmental and cultural variables.

## Geoarchaeology (Background)

- [Butzer — Geoarchaeology and Environmental History](https://sites.utexas.edu/butzer/geoarchaeology-and-environmental-history/) — UT Austin archive. Karl Butzer's foundational work on settlement-environment relationships.
