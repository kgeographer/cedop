# EDOPS analytical use classes / competency questions

## Comparison
- Which places have environmental signatures most similar to place X?
- How environmentally similar are places X and Y?
- Which historical regions occupy comparable environmental settings despite geographic separation?

## Outlier detection
- Which settlements are environmental outliers relative to their size or prominence?
- Which places exhibit unusually high population or complexity for their environmental setting?
- Where do historical outcomes diverge strongly from environmental expectation?

## Trajectory analysis
- How did the environmental signature of place X change over time?
- Did major social or political transitions coincide with environmental shifts?
- How did expansion or migration alter the environmental profile of a polity or population?

## Clustering / typology
- What environmental settlement types emerge from clustering signatures?
- Which places belong to the same environmental settlement or ecology class?
- Can recurrent environmental archetypes of historical societies be identified?

## Affordance / constraint analysis
- What subsistence or economic strategies are environmentally plausible in place X?
- Which environmental constraints would likely shape human activity in place X?
- What opportunities or limitations does this setting present for settlement?

## Boundary / gradient analysis
- Where do sharp environmental transitions occur across a region?
- Do political or cultural boundaries align with environmental discontinuities?
- How rapidly does environmental context change across a frontier or corridor?

## Divergence / dependency analysis
- How dependent is place X on upstream or nonlocal environmental inputs?
- Does local habitability differ substantially from broader catchment conditions?
- Which settlements rely on imported water, fertility, or ecological subsidy?

## Predictive / exploratory modeling
- Which environmental signatures correlate with urbanization or state formation?
- What environmental variables best predict settlement density or complexity?
- Where might similar environmental conditions support analogous historical outcomes?
