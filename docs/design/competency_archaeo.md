## Site suitability / prospecting
- Where are environmentally similar locations to known archaeological sites?
- Which unsurveyed areas exhibit signatures associated with known settlement types?
- Where might archaeologically significant but undiscovered sites be expected?

## Settlement pattern analysis
- How do environmental signatures vary across sites within a settlement hierarchy?
- Do site types cluster in distinct environmental niches?
- How does settlement distribution relate to environmental gradients?

## Land-use reconstruction
- What forms of agriculture or subsistence were environmentally plausible at site X?
- How did surrounding environmental context shape likely land-use strategies?
- What resource zones were accessible from site X?

## Vulnerability / resilience analysis
- Which sites occupied environmentally marginal or risk-prone settings?
- Which settlements were buffered against drought, flood, or resource stress?
- How might environmental context explain settlement persistence or abandonment?

## Mobility / catchment analysis
- What environmental resources lay within practical reach of site X?
- How similar or different are the environmental contexts of linked sites?
- Did exchange/mobility connect environmentally complementary zones?
