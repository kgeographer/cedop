## Cross-cultural correlation
- Which social variables covary with particular environmental signatures?
- Do societies in similar environmental settings exhibit similar cultural traits?
- Which traits remain variable despite environmental similarity?

## Ecological niche comparison
- Which societies occupied comparable environmental niches across different world regions?
- How similar are the environmental contexts of societies with contrasting social forms?
- Are distinct cultural adaptations associated with the same environmental setting?

## Adaptation / strategy analysis
- Which subsistence, mobility, or settlement strategies are associated with particular environmental conditions?
- Under what environmental conditions do mixed economies appear?
- Which cultural strategies recur in highly seasonal, drought-prone, or resource-patchy settings?

## Temporal contextualization
- What reconstructed drought, precipitation, or temperature regime characterized a society’s occupation span?
- Did a society’s observed ethnographic traits persist through stable or unstable climatic intervals?
- How different is the millennium-scale temporal context from the contemporary baseline at that location?

## Environmental exposure / variability
- How variable were climatic conditions over the known occupation span of society X?
- Which societies experienced unusually high hydroclimatic volatility?
- Do particular institutions or practices correlate with long-term environmental instability?

## Cultural divergence under similar conditions
- Which societies differ sharply in social organization despite similar environmental signatures?
- Where does cultural divergence exceed environmental expectation?
- Which cases are best suited for testing non-environmental explanations?

## Environmental constraints on social form
- Which environmental conditions are associated with low population density, mobility, or dispersed settlement?
- Under what conditions do more centralized or stratified forms appear?
- Which environmental variables best delimit the range of observed cultural variation?

## Diffusion / inheritance vs environment
- Are similarities between societies better explained by shared ancestry/contact or by similar environmental context?
- Which traits appear environmentally convergent across unrelated societies?
- Which traits remain clustered by region or lineage despite environmental contrast?
