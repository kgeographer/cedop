EDOPS — Environmental Dimensions of Place Service

EDOPS generates structured environmental signatures for any location on Earth. Signatures are derived from global datasets aggregated at the hydrological sub-basin level — capturing physiography, hydrology, climate, and coastality — with optional historical climate enrichment from paleoclimate reconstructions. EDOPS is the environmental component of the Computing Place research initiative.

The service is available as a researcher sandbox and a REST API. A researcher dashboard is in development.
